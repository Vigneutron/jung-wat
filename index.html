Word association

One hundred stimulus words, in Jung's original order. Answer each with the first word that comes to mind. Reaction time is recorded to the millisecond and also kept in Jung's fifths of a second.

Speech recognition isn't available here. If this page is embedded in a chat, open the file directly in Chrome or Safari to use the microphone. Typing mode works everywhere.

The app itself sends nothing anywhere: your words and timings stay on this page and export as JSON. In mic mode the browser's own speech recognition transcribes your voice, and Chrome and Safari send that audio to Google or Apple servers — choose typing mode to keep everything on this device.

Protocol
#StimulusResponseRT×med

Tap any row to flag it yourself (✱) and note why the word mattered; tap ✎ to fix a misheard response after the run (the original is kept). Your flags, notes, and sieve associations all travel with the export. The ×med column is how many times the median that reaction took. A dot by the number marks a stimulus the source list treats as emotionally loaded. RT runs from the word's delivery to the onset of your answer; the export also carries milliseconds and Jung's fifths of a second. Nothing else is measured, scored, or judged — the reading of this sheet is the reader's.

Associative sieve · choose the words

Which words go under the sieve?

Every marked row from the sheet is listed. Your own flags, blanks, and reactions at twice your median start checked; rows merely above the median start unchecked. Take only the words that pull at you — the sieve rewards depth over coverage.
